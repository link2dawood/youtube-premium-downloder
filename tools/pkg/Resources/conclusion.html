You're set.

The PixelCatch Helper is installed and registered with Chrome.

Next steps

  1. Make sure you're signed in to YouTube in Chrome (and a member or Premium subscriber if you want gated content).
  2. Open chrome://extensions and click the refresh icon on PixelCatch (only needed if it was already running when you installed the helper).
  3. Click the PixelCatch toolbar icon, paste a video URL, pick a quality, and click Download.

Files land in ~/Downloads. Logs are at ~/Library/Logs/com.pixelcatch.downloader.log.
