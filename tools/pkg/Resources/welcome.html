Install the PixelCatch Helper

This installer sets up the small local helper that the PixelCatch Chrome extension needs in order to download YouTube Premium and members-only videos on your Mac.

What gets installed

  • The helper script and a bundled copy of yt-dlp in /Library/Application Support/PixelCatch/
  • A Chrome native messaging registration in /Library/Google/Chrome/NativeMessagingHosts/

What it does not do

  • No remote server, no telemetry, no Google account access.
  • Downloads happen entirely on this Mac, using your existing signed-in YouTube session in Chrome.

You'll need an administrator password to complete the install. After it finishes, reload PixelCatch in chrome://extensions and you're ready to download.
